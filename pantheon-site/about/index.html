Developers, advisory & brokerage, property management

New York–headquartered Pantheon Properties has emerged as one of the leading full-service developers of industrial, commercial/office and development projects.

Our approach

Leadership through integrity

Pantheon Properties has diverse capabilities which include the acquisition, management and development of new building projects, land and rehabilitation opportunities in New Jersey and the New York metro area.

Our broad-based expertise in real estate underscores our flexibility and value as a partner for our JV partners, tenants and investors. Over the past decade, we have built an excellent reputation for identifying opportunities and creating value in a variety of real estate ventures and locations, for our own account as well as third parties. Our broker relationships, quality of construction management, and scrupulous attention to the needs of the tenant, community and environment are unsurpassed.

Our leadership shows in our commitment to partnership, our breadth of services and our delivery of optimal return on investment. Above all, Pantheon prides itself on executing its strategic plan with integrity in every transaction, interaction and approach to new projects.

Partners

Leadership through partnership

Pantheon’s partners include Canyon Capital Realty Advisors, SoBro, Turner Impact Capital and the Turner Multifamily Impact Fund.

24+ year partnership

Pantheon enjoys a 24+ year partnership with Canyon Capital Advisors and has concluded multiple transactions with its affiliate group, The Canyon-Johnson Urban Fund, in the acquisition of urban industrial, office, retail and residential development and redevelopment opportunities.

Our philosophy is simple

By making the real estate process as uncomplicated and accommodating as possible for our tenants, we enable them to focus on and grow their businesses. For our investors, our unwavering commitment to partnership has enabled us to attract a first-class tenant roster, optimize return on investment, and consistently achieve 100 percent occupancy in our properties.

Executive team

Ken Cohen

Founder, President & CEO

Ken Cohen, founder, President and CEO of Pantheon, has been involved in the real estate business for over 36 years, repositioning, remediating, developing and managing properties encompassing millions of square feet throughout the eastern seaboard. His expertise and creativity in property development, management, environmental remediation, energy efficiency and strategic financing are central to Pantheon’s success.
