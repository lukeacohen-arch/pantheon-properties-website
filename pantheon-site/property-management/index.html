Reporting

Financial services

Efficiently managing property finances can be a full-time endeavor, requiring expert understanding of best practices to achieve significant savings. Pantheon is well-equipped for optimizing our clients’ real estate holdings through proven and proactive methods.

Our reporting is clear and straightforward, providing all the information a property owner needs to gauge performance. Pantheon delivers comprehensive financial reporting and forecasting:

  • Monthly statements of operations and annual operating and cash flow budgets
  • Estimated and reconciled CAM amounts
  • Monthly, quarterly or annual reports
  • Rent collection, lease interpretation and enforcement, and accounting services
  • Institutional-level data: current rent rolls, cash flow statements, bank reconciliations, general ledgers and receivables

We also provide frequent updates on tenant delinquencies and recommend legal action only when other efforts have been exhausted, while maintaining positive tenant relationships. Our approach is fair but always firm.

Questions

Property management FAQ

What property management services does Pantheon provide?

Pantheon provides continual and systematic property monitoring and maintenance, value-adding capital improvements, timely response to tenant requests, leasing and marketing services, property maintenance and repair, interior improvements, and energy-efficient retrofits and green construction.

What financial reporting do property owners receive?

Owners receive monthly statements of operations, annual operating and cash flow budgets, estimated and reconciled CAM amounts, and monthly, quarterly or annual reports. Reporting also includes institutional-level data such as current rent rolls, cash flow statements, bank reconciliations, general ledgers and receivables.

How does Pantheon handle tenant delinquencies?

Pantheon provides frequent updates on tenant delinquencies and recommends legal action only when other efforts have been exhausted, while working to maintain positive tenant relationships. Its approach is fair but always firm.
