Who we are

What is Pantheon Properties?

Pantheon Properties is a New York–headquartered developer, advisor, broker and property manager of industrial, commercial/office and land-development real estate in New Jersey and the New York metro area.

We identify opportunities and create value for our own account and for third parties, working as a partner to JV partners, tenants and investors. Our approach pairs broker relationships and construction-management quality with close attention to the needs of the tenant, the community and the environment.

Questions

Pantheon Properties at a glance

What does Pantheon Properties do?

Pantheon Properties is a New York–headquartered, full-service real estate organization. It acquires, develops, brokers and manages industrial, commercial/office and land-development projects in New Jersey and the New York metro area, for its own account and for third parties.

Where are Pantheon Properties’ buildings located?

Pantheon’s current portfolio includes industrial properties in Secaucus, Carlstadt, North Bergen and Newark, New Jersey, and a land opportunity in the Bronx, New York. Its offices are at 119 West 57th Street in Manhattan.

Who leads Pantheon Properties?

Ken Cohen is the founder, President and CEO. He has more than 36 years in real estate, including repositioning, remediating, developing and managing properties across the eastern seaboard.

How do I inquire about available space?

Call 212.277.7500 or email leasing@pantheonproperties.com. Marketing flyers for available units are linked on each property page and in the available-space table on the Properties page.

Who are Pantheon Properties’ partners?

Pantheon’s partners include Canyon Capital Realty Advisors, SoBro, Turner Impact Capital and the Turner Multifamily Impact Fund. Pantheon has had a 24+ year partnership with Canyon Capital Advisors.

Looking for space or a management partner?

Speak with our leasing team about available space, or ask how Pantheon can manage your property.
